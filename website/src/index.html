What Deepika holds to

Six things she works by, with every woman she coaches.

A long, healthy life

The aim is to be strong and independent decades from now. Nobody is chasing a number by a date.

The whole of you

Movement, food, sleep and stress are looked at together, by one person who knows your week.

Slow and sustainable

Change that lasts comes in small pieces. Progress isn't a straight line, and most days is the aim, not every day.

A safe place

No judgement and no blame. A week that went badly is something to understand, never something to apologise for.

You're in the driving seat

You decide where you're going, and what you won't do. Deepika guides, and the plan bends to you.

How it works

It starts with a conversation and goes on in small steps.

  1. 1

    Before you talk

    A few questions about you

    Your health, your routine, what you eat and what you're hoping for. It takes a few minutes, and it means the first conversation can be about you from the start.

  2. 2

    The first conversation · 30 to 45 minutes

    Where you want to be

    You and Deepika talk through what you want, what has got in the way before, and what you can realistically give. You choose one main goal and one alongside it for the next three to six months. No deadline is attached to either.

  3. 3

    Each week

    Two or three small habits

    Each goal becomes a few habits small enough to keep: a walk after dinner, protein at lunch, lights out by eleven. Every habit comes in three sizes, and the smallest one counts in full.

  4. 4

    Each day

    A little, and she can see it

    You tick things off in the app, say how you're feeling in a tap, and message Deepika when something comes up. She has read your week before you next speak.

  5. 5

    Each month, and at six weeks and three months

    Looking again, properly

    Each month you look at the goals together. At six weeks and at three months you repeat a few simple measures of strength, stamina and flexibility, and of how you're sleeping and feeling, and set them beside where you started.

You leave the first conversation with

  • One main goal, and one alongside it
  • Two or three small habits for the first week
  • What is likely to get in the way, and a way round each
  • A time of day for exercise that suits you

Between conversations

The app keeps it going

Everything you and Deepika agree lives in one calm place on your phone. It works in your browser today. There is no wearable to buy and no calorie counting.

  • Today. A short list, and a note from Deepika when there is one.

  • Three sizes. Pick the one that fits the day you're having.

  • Your journey. The phase you're in, and what this week is for.

  • Movement. Only what Deepika has set for you.

The smallest version counts

Every action comes in three sizes: Minimum, Target and Stretch. Doing the minimum is a full win. A tired Tuesday is not a failed Tuesday.

No streaks to lose

You will never watch a counter fall back to zero. Progress is shown as how many of the last 14 days included at least one healthy action, a number that survives a bad week intact.

A person, not a feed

Deepika reads what you log and adjusts your plan. Nothing is written for you in her name, and the app doesn't pretend to be something it isn't.

Your coach

Meet Deepika

{{#COACH_PHOTO}}{{/COACH_PHOTO}}

Deepika is a health coach and personal trainer in India. She works one-to-one with women on strength, energy, sleep and food, through the years when a body, a family and a job all ask for more at once.

The app is the tool, and she is the coach. It keeps your history, shows her what she needs to see, and leaves her time for the conversation that actually helps.

Coaching, not medical care

This is coaching. It doesn't diagnose anything, doesn't interpret test results and doesn't replace your doctor.

If you have a condition such as thyroid, PCOS or diabetes, tell Deepika in the first conversation. She may ask you to check with your doctor before you start.

If something about your health worries you, please see a doctor. Deepika will tell you the same.

Your data is yours

Questions people ask

Who is it for?

Women who want their strength, energy and sleep back, and have a full life to fit that into. It is built around women's bodies and lives, and Deepika works most with women after a baby, through perimenopause and after it. If that isn't you and it still sounds like what you need, ask her.

How much time does it take?

The first conversation is 30 to 45 minutes. After that, a few minutes on most days. Every habit has a smallest version, made for the days when that is all there is.

What happens if I miss a few days?

Nothing bad. A missed day is "not today", never a failure, and there are no streaks to break. When you come back, nothing has reset and there's nothing to catch up on. You carry on from where you are. If you go quiet, Deepika checks in gently, without guilt.

Is this medical advice?

No. It's coaching. It doesn't diagnose, interpret test results or recommend treatment, and it isn't a substitute for a doctor.

I have thyroid, PCOS or diabetes. Can I still do it?

Tell Deepika in the first conversation. She may ask you to get your doctor's go-ahead before you start, and she plans around what your doctor says.

Can I share my blood reports?

If you want to. The app keeps the values together and shows how they move over time. It doesn't interpret them, and neither the app nor Deepika replaces your doctor.

Do I need a gym, or any equipment?

Tell Deepika what you have and where you can train. The sessions are set around that.

Do I need a smartwatch or any other device?

No. You don't need a wearable for any of it.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in the browser on any Android phone or iPhone. A Google Play version is on its way, and you can add it to your home screen in the meantime.
