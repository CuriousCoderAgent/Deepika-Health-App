Built for the weeks that don't go to plan

The smallest version counts

Every action comes in three sizes — Minimum, Target and Stretch. Doing the minimum is a full win, not a consolation prize. A tired Tuesday is not a failed Tuesday.

No streaks to lose

You will never watch a counter fall back to zero. Progress is shown as how many of the last 14 days included at least one healthy action — a number that survives a bad week intact.

A person, not a feed

Deepika reads what you log and adjusts your plan. Nothing is written for you in her name, and the app doesn't pretend to be something it isn't.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us about you

    A few questions: what you want, how much time you really have, and what you won't do. "I won't count calories" is a perfectly good answer, and it's respected.

  2. 2

    Get a 12-week path

    Deepika shapes your plan in three phases — Stabilise, Build, Consolidate — starting small and growing with you.

  3. 3

    Do a little each day

    A short list of actions, a quick check-in on energy, sleep and stress, and movement sessions when they suit you.

  4. 4

    Talk to a person

    Deepika sees your week before you speak, so your time together goes on you rather than on catching up.

Your coach

Meet Deepika

Deepika is a health coach and personal trainer in India, working with women through the changes of midlife — energy, sleep, strength and everything around them. She is the reason this app exists, and she is who you'll hear from.

{{BRAND}} is the tool; she is the coach. The app keeps your history, shows her what she needs to see, and frees up her time for the conversation that actually helps.

Coaching, not medical care

{{BRAND}} doesn't diagnose anything, doesn't interpret test results and doesn't replace your doctor. If something about your health worries you, please see one — Deepika will tell you the same.

Your data is yours

This holds things you tell it about your health, so here is what actually happens to them.

  • Only what you enter is stored, and only because the coaching needs it.
  • Deepika can see it. No other member can — there are no public profiles, feeds or leaderboards.
  • No ads, no selling your data, and no analytics trackers in the app.
  • Your password is stored as a hash that nobody can read back.
  • You can delete your account whenever you like, from inside the app. It removes everything immediately.

Questions people ask

Is this medical advice?

No. It's coaching. {{BRAND}} doesn't diagnose, interpret test results or recommend treatment, and it isn't a substitute for a doctor.

What happens if I miss a few days?

Nothing bad. A missed day is "not today", never a failure — there are no streaks to break. If you go quiet, Deepika checks in gently, without guilt or catch-up.

Do I need a smartwatch or any other device?

No. You don't need a wearable to get value from {{BRAND}}.

Is it only for women in midlife?

It is built with women in midlife in mind — sleep, energy, strength and the changes that come with them. The coaching adapts to you, and anyone ready to start is welcome to.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in the browser on any Android phone or iPhone. A Google Play version is on its way, and you can add it to your home screen in the meantime.

How do I join?

Open the app and create an account. We're starting with a small first group coached by Deepika; if you've been given an invitation code, enter it when you sign up.

Who can see my reports and check-ins?

Deepika, because she is coaching you. Nobody else — not other members, and not advertisers.

How do I delete my account?

Open Your account in the app and choose delete. It takes effect immediately and removes your account and everything in it. Here's the full explanation.
